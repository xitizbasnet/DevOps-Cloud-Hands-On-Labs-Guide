# Lab 02 — Create an Azure Free Trial Account

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This lab guides you through creating an Azure Free Trial account and configuring essential security and cost-management settings before performing Azure-based exercises.

## Objective

Set up Azure with **₹13,300** free credits valid for **30 days**.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure that you have:

* 📧 A Microsoft account (or create one during registration).
* 📱 A mobile phone for identity verification.
* 💳 A valid credit or debit card for account verification.
* 🌐 A stable internet connection.

> [!NOTE]
> Azure uses identity and payment verification during account creation. No charges are incurred unless you upgrade your subscription or exceed the free service limits.

---

## Procedure

### Step 1 — Create an Azure Free Trial Account

1. Navigate to the Azure Free Trial website:

   * **https://azure.microsoft.com/free**
2. Select **Start Free**.

### Step 2 — Sign In

1. Sign in using your existing **Microsoft account**.
2. If you do not have one, create a new Microsoft account.

### Step 3 — Verify Your Identity

1. Verify your identity using your **phone number**.
2. Complete the payment verification using your **credit/debit card**.

### Step 4 — Complete Registration

1. Select **India** as your country/region.
2. Complete the registration process.

### Step 5 — Explore the Azure Portal

1. After registration, the **Azure Portal** opens.
2. Navigate to and explore the **All Services** panel to familiarize yourself with the portal interface.

### Step 6 — Configure a Budget

1. Navigate to:

   ```text
   Cost Management → Budgets → Create Budget
   ```

2. Create a budget with a threshold of:

   ```text
   ₹500
   ```

### Step 7 — Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

1. Navigate to:

   ```text
   Azure Active Directory → My Account → Security Info
   ```

2. Configure **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** for your account.

## Best Practice Tips

> [!TIP]
> Follow these recommendations to maximize your Azure Free Trial credits and maintain a secure environment.

### 💰 Manage Free Credits

* Azure **Free Trial credits expire after 30 days**.
* Plan your lab schedule in advance to make the most of the available credits.

### 🖥️ Optimize Virtual Machine Costs

* Always **deallocate (Stop)** Virtual Machines (VMs) when they are not in use.
* This helps preserve your available credits and avoids unnecessary resource consumption.

### 📊 Monitor Spending

* Use **Azure Cost Analysis** daily to monitor your credit consumption and spending trends.

### 🌏 Choose the Appropriate Region

* For DevOps and learning workloads, use the following regions for lower network latency:

  * **India (South)**
  * **Southeast Asia**
